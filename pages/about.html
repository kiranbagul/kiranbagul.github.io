About me

Leading teams.
Delivering products.

I’m Kiran Bagul, Head of Engineering at Entain, based in Pune. My work combines product-pillar ownership, engineering organisation development, and AI-native transformation, grounded in 18+ years of building software and teams.

The work behind the title.

Today I lead Player Experience engineering at Entain (IVY Comptech), with responsibility for the pillar’s engineering strategy, roadmap, and delivery. I work with product leadership to translate business priorities into engineering plans, coordinate delivery across squads, and manage dependencies with platform and regulatory teams.

I specialise in AI transformation and agentic engineering. I authored the AI-First Delivery Playbook used across market squads and am shaping AI Pods & Ways of Working, covering team topology, capabilities, tooling, and measurement. My technical focus includes LLM systems, agentic workflows, orchestration, evaluation, and safe production rollout.

Previously, I led engineering and the Pune chapter at Bank Jago (Dkatalis Labs), building banking services and growing the chapter from 5 to 40 engineers. Earlier roles took me into logistics platforms at Zalando, reactive systems at Equal Experts, and infrastructure and distributed systems across several industries.

I was a founding member of the William Hill India technology centre, which later became part of Entain. Returning to the organisation has given me the chance to contribute at a different scale.

Outside work, I’m an amateur astronomer, tinkerer, and long-form reader. That interest led me to build SkyWiki and a number of smaller astronomy tools.
